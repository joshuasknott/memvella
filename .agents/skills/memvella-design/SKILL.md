# Memvella Design

Memvella design must prioritize trust, accessibility, emotional safety, and low cognitive load.

## Workflow

1. Use ui-ux-pro-max before visual design. Identify user role: organiser, helper, older adult, or internal operator.
2. Use reference-research only when useful:
   - Mobbin for health, family sharing, permissions, notifications, onboarding, settings, account recovery.
   - Refero for organiser dashboards, memory libraries, insights, marketing, and internal HQ.
   - Godly only for public marketing direction.
3. Use frontend-design for build-ready layout and accessibility checks.
4. Use motion-design only for gentle confirmations or orientation.

## Product Rules

- Plain language beats clever language.
- Large tap targets and readable hierarchy are mandatory.
- Critical alerts, routines, and people states must be obvious.
- Avoid surprise motion, hidden controls, and decorative complexity.
- Distinguish Activity, Alerts, People, Memories, Routines, and Insights clearly.
- Every screen should reduce anxiety or help a family member act.
