# Marketing design

The marketing site has its own lavender and aubergine identity, grounded in the generated reference at `output/marketing-v2/design-reference.png`. The family application retains its existing design.

- Use Figtree for a confident, legible sans serif hierarchy; reserve italic Georgia for the final invitation. Use pale lavender, white and warm ivory section backgrounds, aubergine text, rounded photography and pill actions.
- Lead with the person and product: stories, routines and family connection. Follow with an illustrative companion preview, three setup steps, short FAQs and one waitlist form.
- One consistent conversion action: Join the waitlist. Preserve the existing API and honest validation, loading, success and error feedback. Do not invent launch dates, testimonials or outcome metrics.
- Generated family and garden photographs are illustrative. The companion preview is labelled and offers a keyboard-accessible example conversation dialog; it does not request a microphone or start live voice.
- Keep navigation anchors rooted at `/` for secondary pages. Retain Privacy, Terms and Contact.
- Support 320px phones, tablets and wide desktops without horizontal scrolling. Primary controls have at least 44px touch targets, visible focus and sufficient contrast. Native FAQ disclosures and dialog behavior preserve keyboard access. Respect reduced motion.
- Image generation prompts and source assets are recorded in `output/marketing-v2/image-generation.md`. Optimized WebP files are served from `public/images/`.
