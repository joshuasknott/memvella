# Marketing design

The marketing site follows the selected October 2026 reference at `output/marketing-v3/reference.png`: a warm paper background, oversized Figtree typography, aubergine text and lavender accents, with the companion itself as the main visual. The existing flower wordmark is retained. Shared product colours and controls live in `packages/ui/src/globals.css`; product layouts are documented in `apps/core/design.md`.

- Use large, tightly spaced Figtree headings, generous body text and pill actions. The font is bundled through `next/font/local`, so builds do not depend on Google Fonts. The variable Latin WOFF2 comes from the official Google Fonts distribution; its SIL Open Font License is alongside it in `app/fonts/OFL.txt`.
- Lead with “A little company. A familiar world.” Follow with the working companion example, three setup steps, short FAQs and one waitlist form. Keep the page free of decorative stock or generated family photography.
- One consistent conversion action: Join the waitlist. Preserve the existing API and honest validation, loading, success and error feedback. Do not invent launch dates, testimonials or outcome metrics.
- The companion is rendered as real controls. Visitors can read a fictional memory, try a reminder and explore scripted conversation prompts. A user-initiated 24-second Remotion walkthrough visits the same states, with pause, replay, reset and chapter controls. It stops when hidden or offscreen and does not autoplay or loop. Reduced motion disables scene transitions.
- The optional Read aloud action uses browser speech synthesis and is explicitly labelled example narration. It cancels when leaving the scene or preview. The demonstration does not request a microphone, connect to a model or save any family data.
- Below 850px, the same screen component renders at native text sizes instead of scaling down the fixed desktop composition. Phone layouts stack the memory and routine, while tablet widths retain two columns. Keep keyboard focus meaningful when changing scenes.
- Keep navigation anchors rooted at `/` for secondary pages. Retain Privacy, Terms and Contact.
- Support 320px phones, tablets and wide desktops without horizontal scrolling. Primary actions have at least 44px touch targets, visible focus and sufficient contrast. Native FAQ disclosures preserve keyboard access. Respect reduced motion.
- Current visual evidence and the source comparison are in `output/marketing-v3/`; the review is recorded in the root `design-qa.md`. The social image is captured from the implemented hero. Older illustrative photos remain unused by this homepage.
